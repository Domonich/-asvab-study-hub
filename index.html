🎖️ Your Goal: Army National Guard OCS — GT ≥ 110

Officer Candidate School eligibility runs on your GT (General Technical) line score, not your overall AFQT. GT is built from just three subtests:

  • Arithmetic Reasoning (AR)
  • Word Knowledge (WK)
  • Paragraph Comprehension (PC)

Mathematics Knowledge, General Science, Electronics, Auto & Shop, and Mechanical Comprehension do not factor into GT at all — they matter for enlisted job (MOS) options, not your OCS packet. So while this diagnostic covers all 8 subtests for a full picture, treat AR/WK/PC (tagged GT below) as your priority.

GT is an age/gender-normed standard score (mean 100) computed at MEPS from your actual ASVAB — this site can only give you a directional practice signal from percentage-correct, not your real GT number.

What This Diagnostic Does

The ASVAB (Armed Services Vocational Aptitude Battery) has up to nine subtests. Four of them — Arithmetic Reasoning, Word Knowledge, Paragraph Comprehension, and Mathematics Knowledge — combine to form your AFQT score, the number that determines whether you qualify to enlist at all. The rest determine which military jobs (MOS/ratings) you're eligible for.

This 64-question diagnostic covers 8 of the 9 subtests (everything except Assembling Objects, which needs a visual-puzzle format) so you can see exactly where you're strong and where you need to focus, before you spend time studying blindly.

Note: This gives you a raw percentage-correct per subject as a study guide. It is not an official ASVAB score — the real AFQT is calculated from scaled/percentile scores using the military's own norm tables, not raw percentages.
